About the study tool

Welcome! Use these scenarios to review what you’ve learned in the Kitzur Shulchan Aruch.

This site is a work in progress. The question bank currently covers the first few chapters of the KS"A.

This tool isn't meant to replace opening up a sefer. Rather, once you have a basic grasp of the material, you can strengthen it by having a friend quiz you on what you remember.

This tool was last updated in 2026 and created by Even, who personally wrote all of the question and scenario text. AI was used to help create the web page and functionality, but none of the content.

Special thanks to my friend Yehoshua, who pushed me many years ago to start learning the KS"A.
